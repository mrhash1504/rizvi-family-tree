# Geographic Timeline Corrections (2026-08-14)

## What Was Wrong

The website's geographic timeline had **multiple historical inaccuracies**:

### Previous (Incorrect) Timeline:
```
~1850: Lucknow (Ottoman era)
~1920s: Murshidabad (Early migration)
~1950s: Karachi (Partition era)
~1990s: Dubai/US (Global diaspora)
```

### Problems:
1. **Started at ~1850** — but ancestors documented from c. 1792–1793
2. **"Ottoman era" label** — misleading; should focus on Lucknow family establishment
3. **~1920s without context** — didn't explain *why* they moved (sister married Nawab)
4. **~1950s: Karachi** — inaccurate; family actually went to Dhaka in 1951, post-Partition
5. **Skipped Bangladesh era** — Dhaka settlement (1951–1960s) completely omitted
6. **"Partition era" label** — technically applies to 1951 Dhaka move, not Karachi

## Corrected Timeline

### New (Accurate) Timeline:
```
c. 1792–1793: Lucknow (Lucknow era)
~1927–1928: Murshidabad (Sister marries Nawab)
~1951: Dhaka (Post-Partition diaspora)
~1960s–1970s: Karachi (Pakistan migration)
~1980s–2000s: Dubai/US (Global diaspora)
```

### Why Each Point:

**c. 1792–1793: Lucknow (Lucknow era)**
- Mir Ali Hussain Rizvi born in Lucknow
- Earliest confirmed ancestor with documented name
- Family established in Mohalla Nakkhas, Lucknow
- Held land in Aaram Ganj, Lucknow

**~1927–1928: Murshidabad (Sister marries Nawab)**
- Syed Hussain Ali Rizvi's sister married into a Nawab family whose title ended in "Jah"
- Documented Nawab families: Nawab Ali Jah (r. 1810–1821), Nawab Walla Jah (r. 1821–1824), Nawab Feradun Jah (r. 1838–1880)
- Family relocated to follow the sister (common practice for close relatives)
- Owned property in Ikraam Ganj, Murshidabad
- Refurbished a mosque there (name not recorded)

**~1951: Dhaka (Post-Partition diaspora)**
- Syed Hussain Ali Rizvi died c. 1950 in Murshidabad
- Family relocated to Dhaka, Bangladesh c. 1951
- This was immediate post-Partition era (1947)
- Bangladesh (East Pakistan, now independent) was part of Partition diaspora

**~1960s–1970s: Karachi (Pakistan migration)**
- Later migration from Bangladesh to Karachi, Pakistan
- Part of ongoing family spread

**~1980s–2000s: Dubai/US (Global diaspora)**
- Global spread to UAE, USA, UK, Canada
- Current generation living across multiple continents
