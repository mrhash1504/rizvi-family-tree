# Rizvi Family History & Ancestors

**Compiled by**: Hussain Raza  
**Source**: Family conversation and oral tradition  
**Verification Status**: Mixed — some facts confirmed against historical records, others remain family lore  
**Living Records**: 104 people documented; tree spans from c. 1650 AD to 2026

---

## Earliest Ancestor & Oral Tradition

**Claimed Lineage**: Family holds an oral tradition of descent from **Imam Ali al-Rida** (the 8th Imam, 766–818 AD) through an ancestor named **Musa al-Mubarqa**.

**Reality**: No documented genealogical link exists between this claimed ancestor and Mir Ali Hussain Rizvi (the earliest ancestor with a confirmed name). The ~1,000-year gap remains unverified. This is a common tradition among Sadaat families and is kept as family lore, not established fact.

**Note**: This is a credible oral tradition—many Sayyid families hold similar claims—but lacks documentary proof.

---

## The Samana Tradition (Unverified)

The family recalls a village called **Samana** (in the Patiala area of Punjab) with four sub-villages, where three brothers who migrated from Iran settled in different parts of India.

**One of these brothers**: Mir Amanullah, possibly the first Sayyid of the line to migrate from Iran to Samana.

**Interesting Corroboration**: 
- A real town called Samana exists on the Samana–Patiala road in Punjab
- It is independently documented in historical records as linked to **Sayyid Mash'had Ali** (son of the 8th Imam), buried there
- This alignment with family tradition is noteworthy but coincidental—no public record of "Mir Amanullah" specifically has been found

**Status**: Family history, not verified genealogy. Worth investigating if family wants to trace earlier roots.

## The Shajra (Photographed 2024)

A handwritten family tree titled **"Rizvi Sadaat (Lucknow)"** was photographed and shared.

### What the shajra shows:
- Opens with Imam Ali al-Rida (766–818 AD)
- Jumps to c. 1650 AD with no bridging generations (~800-year gap)
- Readable lineage from c. 1650 onwards:
  - **Mir Syed Ali Hussain Rizvi** (c. 1650 AD)
  - → **Mir Syed Ghulam Raza Rizvi**
  - → **Syed Afzal Hussain Rizvi** (of Lucknow, m. Syeda Fatima Begum)
  - → **Syed Hussain Ali Rizvi** (dated 1870–1950, three wives listed)

### Additional shajra notes:
- Family lived in **Mohalla Nakkhas** (Nakkhas), Lucknow
- Ancestor was a religious scholar (aalim) and zakir (reciter of elegies)
- Family held land in **Aaram Ganj**, Lucknow

### Handwriting caveat:
Several names were read from difficult handwriting. Treat everything from the shajra as provisional until a fluent reader verifies against the original document.

## Family Confirmed Details (After Shajra Was Shared)

The family has since confirmed:

1. **Generation order**: The shajra's order (Mir Ghulam Raza Rizvi before Syed Afzal Hussain Rizvi) is **correct**. Family's earlier recollection had these two reversed.

2. **Syed Ghulam Raza Rizvi correction**: 
   - The "Ghulam Raza" in the shajra as child of Syed Hussain Ali Rizvi = **Hussain Raza's father**
   - Shajra death year (2013) is **incorrect** → actually died **1 January 2015** in Dubai
   - His wife's full name: **Tasneem Fatima, of Naqvi descent** (shajra shortened it to "Tasneem Naqvi")

3. **Syed Hussain Ali Rizvi's wives**:
   - **1st wife**: Mother of Syed Ali Rizvi; passed away shortly after his birth
   - **2nd wife**: Sister of the 1st wife; passed away shortly after her own childbirth (likely mother of Yousuf Jahan)
   - **3rd wife**: **Nowrozi Begum** (daughter of Sheikh Munawwar Ali); mother of remaining children including Syed Ghulam Raza Rizvi

4. **Birth year estimates**: Family estimates place Syed Hussain Ali Rizvi's birth at **1882–1883**, not the shajra's 1870

## How Birth Years Were Calculated

**Methodology**: No documented dates—all are estimates worked backward from:
- Living memories of age gaps between siblings
- Ages at death (confirmed from recent records)
- Marriage ages and spouse ages

### Example calculation for Syed Hussain Ali Rizvi:
- Family recalls he married Nowrozi Begum c. 1927–1928 at age 45
- Nowrozi Begum was age 15 at that marriage
- So: 1927/1928 − 45 = birth year **1882–1883**
- And Nowrozi Begum: 1927/1928 − 15 = birth year **1912–1913**

### This method's accuracy:
- Good when siblings' age gaps are recalled consistently
- Less reliable the further back you go
- All estimates remain **working figures**, not confirmed dates

## The Great Migration: Lucknow to Murshidabad to Dhaka

### Why Lucknow to Murshidabad?

**Family memory**: Syed Hussain Ali Rizvi moved from Lucknow to Murshidabad because **his sister married into a Nawab family there whose title ended in "Jah"**.

**Historical corroboration** ✅:
- The Nawab Nazims (later Nawab Bahadurs) of Murshidabad were a Shia Muslim dynasty
- They used "Jah" as a recurring honorific title:
  - **Nawab Ali Jah** (r. 1810–1821)
  - **Nawab Walla Jah** (r. 1821–1824)
  - **Nawab Feradun Jah** (r. 1838–1880, last Nawab of Bengal)
- A marriage between a Shia Sayyid family (Rizvis) and this Shia Nawabi family is plausible and fits the timeline
- **Specific individual not yet identified** — this remains a promising lead for future research

### Property in Murshidabad:
- Owned land in **Ikraam Ganj**, Murshidabad (distinct from Lucknow's Aaram Ganj)
- **Refurbished a mosque** there (name not yet recorded)

### Burial in Murshidabad:
- Buried at a site the family calls **"Hussaini Dalan"**
- Note: A famous Hussaini Dalan Imambara exists in Dhaka, Bangladesh, but that is almost certainly not this one (he was based in Murshidabad, not Dhaka)
- Treat as a distinct, locally-named Murshidabad site until confirmed

### After his death (~1950):
- **Relocation to Dhaka** c. 1951
- Family moved to Bangladesh in the immediate post-Partition era

## Earlier Ancestors in Lucknow

- **Residence**: Lucknow

### Burial site clue:
- **Talkatora Karbala** — a major Shia burial ground in Nakkhas quarter, Lucknow
- Built c. 1800–1817 by Mir Khuda Bakhsh (revenue official under Nawab Saadat Ali Khan)
- Is a plausible candidate for where Syed Afzal Hussain Rizvi and earlier ancestors are buried
- **Has NOT been confirmed** which specific site or grave
